# Paul Graham essays — filtered reading lists

Source: [paulgraham.com/articles.html](https://www.paulgraham.com/articles.html) — all 233 essays read and rated, 24 Oct 2026.

Two lists, two readers. Essays not on either list (~170) are VC/YC/fundraising mechanics, startup hiring and equity, programming languages, or politics.

---

## List 1 — Newbie hustler / independent solo earner (not Silicon Valley)

### Start here
1. [Do Things that Don't Scale](https://www.paulgraham.com/ds.html) — how to get your first clients: by hand, one at a time, over-deliver.
2. [Haters](https://www.paulgraham.com/fh.html) — anyone with a public profile gets them; why, and why you don't engage.
3. [Write Simply](https://www.paulgraham.com/simply.html) — for ads, profiles, messages.
4. [How to Lose Time and Money](https://www.paulgraham.com/selfindulgence.html) — new cash disappears through bad "investments," not spending.
5. [The Right Kind of Stubborn](https://www.paulgraham.com/persistence.html) — hold the goal, change the tactics.
6. [Life is Short](https://www.paulgraham.com/vb.html) — permission to fire bad clients and skip online fights.

### Getting and keeping clients
- [Be Good](https://www.paulgraham.com/good.html) — "what's actually best for the client" as your default rule; it drives referrals.
- [The Ronco Principle](https://www.paulgraham.com/ronco.html) — referral work runs on reputation; today's nobody is tomorrow's big client.
- [Two Kinds of Judgement](https://www.paulgraham.com/judgement.html) — a client passing on you isn't a verdict on you; treat it as selling.
- [Making Startups Powerful](https://www.paulgraham.com/powerful.html) — ignore the title: own your client relationships, don't let a platform own them.
- [Schlep Blindness](https://www.paulgraham.com/schlep.html) — the unglamorous work nobody wants is where the money is.
- [What Doesn't Seem Like Work?](https://www.paulgraham.com/work.html) — what others dread but you don't mind is your niche.

### Money
- [Stuff](https://www.paulgraham.com/stuff.html) — don't convert new income into junk you'll never use.
- [Ramen Profitable](https://www.paulgraham.com/ramenprofitable.html) — cover your own living costs first; then nobody can pressure you.
- [Mind the Gap](https://www.paulgraham.com/gap.html) — you're paid for what buyers want, not hours; stop apologizing for earning.
- [How to Make Wealth](https://www.paulgraham.com/wealth.html) — skip the startup half; wealth is made by giving people what they want, and solo work means your effort shows directly in your income.
- [You Weren't Meant to Have a Boss](https://www.paulgraham.com/boss.html) — self-employment is the natural state; never run on credit-card debt.
- [The Top Idea in Your Mind](https://www.paulgraham.com/top.html) — money worries and feuds hijack your thinking; don't let them.

### Writing ads, profiles, messages
- [Write Like You Talk](https://www.paulgraham.com/talk.html) — one test for every line you write.
- [Writing, Briefly](https://www.paulgraham.com/writing44.html) — the whole method in one paragraph.
- [How to Write Usefully](https://www.paulgraham.com/useful.html) — make the strongest claim that's still true; drop the hedging.
- [How to Present to Investors](https://www.paulgraham.com/investors.html) — ignore the title: lead with what you do, show don't tell, no bravado. Surprisingly good profile-copy advice.
- [The Brand Age](https://www.paulgraham.com/brandage.html) — in a crowded market distinctiveness sells, but quality still has to clear the bar.

### Haters, stigma, criticism
- [What You Can't Say](https://www.paulgraham.com/say.html) — living outside the mainstream: think freely, pick your battles, keep a trusted circle.
- [Keep Your Identity Small](https://www.paulgraham.com/identity.html) — so criticism of the work isn't an attack on you, and the work doesn't become your whole self.
- [How to Disagree](https://www.paulgraham.com/disagree.html) — a ranking of criticism: answer substance, ignore insults and tone-policing.
- [Mean People Fail](https://www.paulgraham.com/mean.html) — don't stop to fight; race ahead.
- [See Randomness](https://www.paulgraham.com/randomness.html) — a slow month or a rude client is usually noise, not a message.
- [The Power of the Marginal](https://www.paulgraham.com/marginal.html) — no credentials, no backing, outsider: that's an edge, not a defect.

### Persistence
- [The Anatomy of Determination](https://www.paulgraham.com/determination.html) — with no boss, your discipline is the only check.
- [How to Work Hard](https://www.paulgraham.com/hwh.html) — nobody sets your hours now; find your real limit, don't fake busy.
- [Relentlessly Resourceful](https://www.paulgraham.com/relres.html) — when blocked, try another angle instead of waiting.
- [How Not to Die](https://www.paulgraham.com/die.html) — most failures are just quitting quietly.
- [Being a Noob](https://www.paulgraham.com/noob.html) — feeling clueless means you're learning, not failing.
- [Early Work](https://www.paulgraham.com/early.html) — your first ads, prices and clients will look amateur; judge by improvement.

### Time, attention, sanity
- [Good and Bad Procrastination](https://www.paulgraham.com/procrastination.html) — errands vs. real work; the to-do-list trap.
- [Disconnecting Distraction](https://www.paulgraham.com/distraction.html) — make distractions physically inconvenient.
- [The Acceleration of Addictiveness](https://www.paulgraham.com/addiction.html) — set your own rules for your attention; looking weird is fine.
- [Maker's Schedule, Manager's Schedule](https://www.paulgraham.com/makersschedule.html) — batch calls and admin into fixed windows.
- [The Top of My Todo List](https://www.paulgraham.com/todo.html) — five-line sanity checklist.
- [How to Do What You Love](https://www.paulgraham.com/love.html) — the "earn now, fund what you love later" route is legit.
- [How to Do Great Work](https://www.paulgraham.com/greatwork.html) — long, but it's the general manual: morale, small loyal audience, cutting out middlemen.

### Worth it if you can tune out the startup-speak
- [Default Alive or Default Dead?](https://www.paulgraham.com/aord.html) — monthly check: does income cover costs with no rescue coming?
- [The Fatal Pinch](https://www.paulgraham.com/pinch.html) — cash getting tight: cut costs now, do what brings money soonest.
- [Startups in 13 Sentences](https://www.paulgraham.com/13sentences.html) — delight a few, give surprisingly good service, never count on a deal until it closes.
- [Why Smart People Have Bad Ideas](https://www.paulgraham.com/bronze.html) — keep asking "will people pay?"; selling, pricing, bookkeeping are learnable chores.
- [What Startups Are Really Like](https://www.paulgraham.com/really.html) — what working for yourself actually feels like: swings, slowness, no respect.
- [The Lesson to Unlearn](https://www.paulgraham.com/lesson.html) — the only test that counts is whether clients want you and recommend you.
- [A Word to the Resourceful](https://www.paulgraham.com/word.html) — when someone gives you advice, follow it through instead of filtering it through decisions you've already made.
- [Superlinear Returns](https://www.paulgraham.com/superlinear.html) — reputation compounds; the flat early stretch is where people quit.
- [Putting Ideas into Words](https://www.paulgraham.com/words.html) — self-edit by rereading as a skeptical stranger.
- [The Submarine](https://www.paulgraham.com/submarine.html) — ask who benefits before trusting hype or a pitch.
- [When To Do What You Love](https://www.paulgraham.com/when.html) — honest about money vs. passion.
- [How to Think for Yourself](https://www.paulgraham.com/think.html) — ask "is that true?" about everything you're told.
- [Why Nerds are Unpopular](https://www.paulgraham.com/nerds.html) — why outsiders get attacked: it's bonding, not about you.
- [The Airbnbs](https://www.paulgraham.com/airbnbs.html) — cover living costs first, pick the eager customers, better photos.

## List 2 — Quant finance: researcher or trader

### Start here
1. [How to Think for Yourself](https://www.paulgraham.com/think.html) — PG's own example is investors: a correct view nobody else holds is the only kind that pays. The alpha essay.
2. [How to Do Great Work](https://www.paulgraham.com/greatwork.html) — a manual for research careers: choosing problems, curiosity, morale, working daily.
3. [See Randomness](https://www.paulgraham.com/randomness.html) + [A Way to Detect Bias](https://www.paulgraham.com/bias.html) — noise vs. signal, and a clean selection-bias argument.
4. [Keep Your Identity Small](https://www.paulgraham.com/identity.html) — don't marry a position.
5. [Superlinear Returns](https://www.paulgraham.com/superlinear.html) — power laws, thresholds, compounding, in careers and in returns.
6. [When To Do What You Love](https://www.paulgraham.com/when.html) — PG on choosing money over passion, and when that's fine. Your exact decision.

### Thinking, randomness, ideas
- [Black Swan Farming](https://www.paulgraham.com/swan.html) — framed as VC, but it's about fat tails: a few winners pay for everything, and the best ideas look wrong at the time.
- [The Lesson to Unlearn](https://www.paulgraham.com/lesson.html) — "hacking the test" is overfitting; live PnL is the test you can't hack.
- [How to Get New Ideas](https://www.paulgraham.com/getideas.html) — anomalies only show up once you know a field deeply. That's alpha research.
- [Beyond Smart](https://www.paulgraham.com/smart.html) — everyone at the firm is smart; what separates the ones who find things.
- [Fierce Nerds](https://www.paulgraham.com/fn.html) — competitive, overconfident, impatient nerds and what to aim that at; includes the bitterness failure mode.
- [How to Be an Expert in a Changing World](https://www.paulgraham.com/ecw.html) — hold models loosely; regimes change.
- [Fashionable Problems](https://www.paulgraham.com/fp.html) — crowded problems vs. unexplored corners. Crowded trades.
- [Is It Worth Being Wise?](https://www.paulgraham.com/wisdom.html) — high average vs. rare peaks; a trader needs the high average.
- [Coronavirus and Credibility](https://www.paulgraham.com/cred.html) — make checkable predictions and keep score.
- [The Risk of Discovery](https://www.paulgraham.com/disc.html) — survivorship bias in judging what good bets looked like at the time.
- [What You Can't Say](https://www.paulgraham.com/say.html) — the original contrarian-thinking essay: how to notice what everyone assumes.

### Work, persistence, craft
- [How to Work Hard](https://www.paulgraham.com/hwh.html) — find your real limit; don't fake busy.
- [The Right Kind of Stubborn](https://www.paulgraham.com/persistence.html) — hold the goal, change the tactics; treat objections as information.
- [The Anatomy of Determination](https://www.paulgraham.com/determination.html) — willfulness plus discipline aimed at a worthy goal.
- [Good and Bad Procrastination](https://www.paulgraham.com/procrastination.html) — errands vs. real work.
- [Maker's Schedule, Manager's Schedule](https://www.paulgraham.com/makersschedule.html) — research needs half-day blocks; one meeting can wreck a day.
- [Disconnecting Distraction](https://www.paulgraham.com/distraction.html) — make distractions physically inconvenient.
- [The Acceleration of Addictiveness](https://www.paulgraham.com/addiction.html) — set your own rules for your attention.
- [How to Disagree](https://www.paulgraham.com/disagree.html) — the hierarchy of argument, for research debates.
- [Being a Noob](https://www.paulgraham.com/noob.html) — feeling clueless means you're learning.
- [Early Work](https://www.paulgraham.com/early.html) — your first research will look lame; judge by rate of improvement.
- [The Top Idea in Your Mind](https://www.paulgraham.com/top.html) — PnL swings and office politics hijack your thinking; don't let them.
- [Life is Short](https://www.paulgraham.com/vb.html) — cut what isn't worth it.

### Writing research
- [How to Write Usefully](https://www.paulgraham.com/useful.html) — correct, important, novel, strong; say the boldest true thing.
- [Write Simply](https://www.paulgraham.com/simply.html) — plain words expose empty claims, including your own.
- [Persuade xor Discover](https://www.paulgraham.com/discover.html) — write to find out what's true, not to win the argument.
- [Putting Ideas into Words](https://www.paulgraham.com/words.html) — writing an idea down exposes the gaps you didn't know were there.

### Money and career
- [Two Kinds of Judgement](https://www.paulgraham.com/judgement.html) — interview rejections aren't verdicts on you; firms are selecting for fit.
- [After Credentials](https://www.paulgraham.com/credentials.html) — credentials get you in the door; measurement decides everything after.
- [What You'll Wish You'd Known](https://www.paulgraham.com/hs.html) — if you're still a student: "stay upwind" means do the math.
- [How to Do What You Love](https://www.paulgraham.com/love.html) — the prestige and money traps, named.
- [How to Make Wealth](https://www.paulgraham.com/wealth.html) — the "measured and leveraged" frame fits trading better than almost any job.
- [Mind the Gap](https://www.paulgraham.com/gap.html) — PG's case for why high pay for high-value work is legitimate.
- [How to Lose Time and Money](https://www.paulgraham.com/selfindulgence.html) — new money disappears through things that look like responsible investments.
- [Stuff](https://www.paulgraham.com/stuff.html) — don't convert new income into junk.

### If you'll build systems
- [Holding a Program in One's Head](https://www.paulgraham.com/head.html) — complex code needs long uninterrupted stretches.
- [Taste for Makers](https://www.paulgraham.com/taste.html) — simplicity, solving the right problem, redesigning often.
- [Beating the Averages](https://www.paulgraham.com/avg.html) — edge from better tools; the Blub paradox.

### Worth it if you can tune out the startup-speak
- [Crazy New Ideas](https://www.paulgraham.com/newideas.html) — when a credible person proposes something absurd-sounding, ask why.
- [Novelty and Heresy](https://www.paulgraham.com/nov.html) — good ideas hide behind mistaken assumptions.
- [The Four Quadrants of Conformism](https://www.paulgraham.com/conformism.html) — independent-minded vs. conventional, mapped.
- [The Bus Ticket Theory of Genius](https://www.paulgraham.com/genius.html) — obsessive interest aimed at something that matters.
- [General and Surprising](https://www.paulgraham.com/sun.html) — valuable ideas are general and surprising.
- [The Two Kinds of Moderate](https://www.paulgraham.com/mod.html) — judge each question on its merits.
- [Cities and Ambition](https://www.paulgraham.com/cities.html) — New York, Chicago, London each broadcast a different ambition.
- [The Submarine](https://www.paulgraham.com/submarine.html) — read financial media knowing who planted the story.
- [Good Writing](https://www.paulgraham.com/goodwriting.html) — prose that sounds right usually carries right ideas.
- [The Need to Read](https://www.paulgraham.com/read.html) and [How You Know](https://www.paulgraham.com/know.html) — reading as the substrate of thinking.
- [Great Hackers](https://www.paulgraham.com/gh.html) and [The Python Paradox](https://www.paulgraham.com/pypar.html) — what a firm's tool choices signal about who works there.
- [Hackers and Painters](https://www.paulgraham.com/hp.html) — makers: sketch, iterate, empathize with the user.
- [Undergraduation](https://www.paulgraham.com/college.html) — for students: get great at the craft, find smart people, build things.
- [A Project of One's Own](https://www.paulgraham.com/own.html) — self-directed work vs. assigned work.
- [Inequality and Risk](https://www.paulgraham.com/inequality.html) and [Economic Inequality](https://www.paulgraham.com/ineq.html) — PG's risk-and-return case on inequality.
- [How People Get Rich Now](https://www.paulgraham.com/richnow.html) and [How to Earn a Billion Dollars](https://www.paulgraham.com/earn.html) — growth rate × duration.
- [Schlep Blindness](https://www.paulgraham.com/schlep.html) — the unglamorous data work everyone avoids.
- [What Doesn't Seem Like Work?](https://www.paulgraham.com/work.html) — research vs. trading: which chores don't feel like chores to you?

### Fun math, zero career value
- [Modeling a Wealth Tax](https://www.paulgraham.com/wtax.html)
- [How to Convert Between Wealth and Income Tax](https://www.paulgraham.com/winc.html)
