## The steps (all-in on quant, one path)

**Foundation — non-negotiable, path-agnostic:**
- [ ] **Ace STA347 (Probability) + MAT337 (Real Analysis)** — also STA302, CSC263. #1 priority: fixes your transcript *and* is interview prep in one.
- [ ] **Green Book (Zhou)** — drill alongside STA347. Your single most important interview drill. (It's already in your `/QUANT/` folder.)
- [ ] **Zetamac** daily → hit **50+** per game (mental-math screen).

**Signal — how you stand out with no network:**
- [ ] **One competition, finished and ranked.** Priority: Optiver/Jane Street **Kaggle** → IMC Prosperity (spring) → WorldQuant BRAIN → **Putnam** (Dec) → Rotman RITC (Feb). *One real placement beats five half-entries.*
The literal, named ones. These are the platforms where a stranger becomes a candidate:

| Leaderboard | What it is | Why it's your move |
|---|---|---|
| **Kaggle — firm-hosted comps** (Jane Street, Optiver, Two Sigma) | These firms *host their own* Kaggle competitions with public/private leaderboards. Recent examples: *Jane Street Real-Time Market Data*, *Optiver Trading at the Close / Realized Volatility Prediction*. | The firm is **literally the host** — they watch the top of the board and email people. Matches your pandas/ML skill exactly. **Highest ROI for you.** |
| **WorldQuant BRAIN + IQC** (International Quant Championship) | A platform where you build "alphas" (signals) that get scored and ranked; WorldQuant pays for good ones and runs a global championship. | **Designed as a recruiting funnel**, hires internationally (visa-agnostic). Built for exactly your no-network situation. |
| **IMC Prosperity** | IMC Trading's massive annual **algorithmic-trading competition**, global leaderboard, runs ~spring. Thousands of students. | Open to anyone worldwide, explicit recruiting pipeline, beginner-accessible. Great first one. |
| **Citadel / Citadel Securities Datathon** (via Correlation One) | Regional/virtual data competitions; **winners are fast-tracked to interviews.** | Direct "win → interview" path, no connection needed. |
| **Numerai / Quantiacs / QuantConnect** | Numerai = crowdsourced hedge fund; you submit models to a weekly tournament with a leaderboard and get paid. Quantiacs/QuantConnect = quant strategy contests. | Continuous (weekly), ML-friendly, builds a public track record. |
| **Putnam** (first Saturday of December) | The hardest undergrad math competition; scores are ranked nationally. | Not "algo" but firms *absolutely* check Putnam scores. Pure math signal. |
| **Rotman RITC** (U of T's own, ~February) | The Rotman International Trading Competition — hosted **at your school**. | Local, accessible, and a networking + signal twofer. Do this one. |
| **Codeforces rating** (only if targeting quant *dev*) | Competitive-programming rating recruiters filter by. | Skip unless you go the dev route. |

**How "watching the leaderboard" actually works:** for firm-hosted comps (Kaggle, WorldQuant, IMC, Citadel Datathon), the firm runs it *to source candidates* — top finishers get contacted or auto-advanced. For open platforms (Kaggle tier, Codeforces, Numerai), recruiters **search by rank/tier**, and you put the result on your resume/LinkedIn as a hard credential.

**Your priority order:** 1) an **Optiver/Jane Street Kaggle** comp (fits your skills, firm is watching), 2) **IMC Prosperity** (spring, easy entry), 3) **WorldQuant BRAIN** (ongoing, visa-friendly), 4) **Putnam** (December), 5) **Rotman RITC** (February, local).

Pick **one** to actually finish and rank in — a single real placement beats five half-entries.
- [ ] **One flagship project = your competition entry.** The Kaggle vol comp *is* the project (big-data + pandas + ML = your strength). Then, if you have runway, the **satellite-imagery alpha** built on your coordinate-to-image skill — the thing almost no one else can build.
- [ ] **Cold-email a U of T prof for a research RA** — pitch the satellite-alpha as the research. This fuses your publication + project + credential into one line.

**Network — secondary, and only *after* you have the package:**
- [ ] **LinkedIn → U of T alumni at target firms** for referrals/coffee chats. A referral moves a strong package to the top of the pile; it can't replace the package.

**The decision:** all-in on quant this cycle. Drones are deferred, not lost — the same grades/project/skills pivot to commercial drone companies later at near-zero cost if quant stalls.
