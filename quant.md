## The steps (all-in on quant, one path)

**Foundation — non-negotiable, path-agnostic:**
- [ ] **Ace STA347 (Probability) + MAT337 (Real Analysis)** — also STA302, CSC263. #1 priority: fixes your transcript *and* is interview prep in one.
- [ ] **Green Book (Zhou)** — drill alongside STA347. Your single most important interview drill. (It's already in your `/QUANT/` folder.)
- [ ] **Zetamac** daily → hit **50+** per game (mental-math screen).

**Signal — how you stand out with no network:**
- [ ] **One competition, finished and ranked.** Priority: Optiver/Jane Street **Kaggle** → IMC Prosperity (spring) → WorldQuant BRAIN → **Putnam** (Dec) → Rotman RITC (Feb). *One real placement beats five half-entries.*
- [ ] **One flagship project = your competition entry.** The Kaggle vol comp *is* the project (big-data + pandas + ML = your strength). Then, if you have runway, the **satellite-imagery alpha** built on your coordinate-to-image skill — the thing almost no one else can build.
- [ ] **Cold-email a U of T prof for a research RA** — pitch the satellite-alpha as the research. This fuses your publication + project + credential into one line.

**Network — secondary, and only *after* you have the package:**
- [ ] **LinkedIn → U of T alumni at target firms** for referrals/coffee chats. A referral moves a strong package to the top of the pile; it can't replace the package.

**The decision:** all-in on quant this cycle. Drones are deferred, not lost — the same grades/project/skills pivot to commercial drone companies later at near-zero cost if quant stalls.
